About the owner:

The owner(Cial Hendricks) first began his career in 2024 as a flourist in Constania working for a local flouristing company which provides bouquets for different events and occassions. He was quiet good at making different flower arrangements and the owner was quiet impressed with is skills, one day a customer came into the store to buy a couple of bouquets for a wedding that was coming up. As Cial was busy making the bouquets he overheard the customer also speaking about getting candy bouquets as well but had no idea where they would get them from, suddenly Cial had an idea and told the customer that he could make the candy bouquets as well since he knew how to do flower bouquets. The customer was quiet happy that they had found someone to make the candy bouquets and allowwed Cial to make the candy bouquets, later that evening Cial quickly ran to Checkers to get the supplys for the bouquet per the customers request. The day of the wedding had came and Cial had delivered and set up both arrangements before any of the people had arrived, later that day he had a call from the customer who bought the bouquets telling him that the people loved his bouquet designs and that they would buy themseleves these bouquets as well. From that day on Cial had grown his side bouquet business into a real thriving business and is always coming up with new ideas and better bouquets to sell to the world.
